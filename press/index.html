Press

TL;DR

Dela is a minimalist iOS puzzle game about dividing shapes into perfect halves. 34 hand-crafted levels plus a daily puzzle and an unlockable endless mode. Available on iOS 17 and later. Premium upfront: one price, no ads, no IAP, no subscription.

Assets

The press kit is an archive containing the app icon (1024×1024), 5 to 10 in-game screenshots at iPhone 6.7" resolution, the App Preview video (if produced), the wordmark in PNG and SVG, a 200-word "about" paragraph in English, and high-res photos of the maker.
