Press

TL;DR

Dela is a puzzle game about dividing. Draw one continuous line along the grid, edge to edge, bends allowed, and cut a shape in two. In chapter I the halves must hold the same area. In chapter II they must be twins: the same shape, turned or mirrored. Get it right and the gold half lifts, turns and lands on its ivory twin. Get it wrong and Dela shows you why.

Dela 2.0 arrives at the end of September 2026 as a free update. It is free to try, with one purchase to unlock the rest. No ads, no subscription, no login.
