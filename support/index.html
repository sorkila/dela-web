Support

If something is wrong with Dela (a bug, a board that won’t load, a purchase that didn’t go through, a question about how something works), email hello@sorkila.com. We read every email and reply within a few days.

Playing

How do I draw a line?
Put your finger on an edge of the shape and draw one continuous line along the grid to another edge. Bend it wherever you like. Lift your finger to cut.
What counts as a right line?
In chapter I, Halves, both parts must have the same area. In chapter II, Twins, both parts must also be the same shape. Turned or mirrored still counts.
My line was wrong. What happened?
Dela shows you why. The gold half tries to land on the other one, the cells that don’t fit glow, and it slides back. Draw again.
How do hints work?
When you have been stuck for a while, a small mark appears at the bottom of the screen. Tap it for a nudge. Each nudge shows a little more, up to the whole line. To change how soon hints are offered, open home (the grid icon at the top) and set hints to Off, Patient or Eager under Feel.
Where is the original game?
Open home and tap The original Dela under Classic. Drag across the shape to cut along a grid line, tap a cut to remove it, and tap Back to undo. Two parts of equal area, with as few cuts as possible.

Purchases

What is free?
Dela is free to download, and chapters I and II are free to play. One purchase unlocks the rest: the original Dela and the later chapters. The supporter option unlocks the same.
I bought Dela before. Do I have to pay again?
No. Everything is already yours on the same Apple Account, including the original Dela and the later chapters. If something shows as locked, tap “Restore purchase” on the unlock screen.
How do I restore my purchase?
Open home, tap The original Dela, and tap “Restore purchase” on the unlock screen. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought Dela with.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases go through Apple, so refunds do too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Can I sync my progress between devices?
Not currently. Dela stores progress on the device only. Your purchase belongs to your Apple Account, so you can restore it on any device.
What happens to my data when I delete the app?
All progress is removed. Your purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored.
