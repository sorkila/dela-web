Support

If something is wrong with Dela (a bug, a level that won’t load, a question about how something works), email hello@sorkila.com. We read every email and reply within a few days.

Common questions

How do I make a cut?
Drag your finger across the shape. The cut snaps to the nearest horizontal or vertical grid line. Tap a cut to remove it.
How do I undo?
Tap “back” at the bottom of the screen.
Can I sync my progress between devices?
Not currently. Dela stores progress on the device only.
What happens to my data when I delete the app?
All progress is removed.
