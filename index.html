How it plays

The mechanic is simple. The decisions are not.

  1. 1One line

    Draw it along the grid, from edge to edge. Bend it where you like. Lift your finger and the shape parts.

  2. 2Two halves

    It begins with equal halves. In chapter I the parts only have to hold the same area, whatever their shape.

  3. 3Twins

    In chapter II they have to match, turned or mirrored. The gold half lifts, turns and lands on the ivory one, with a thud and a little gold dust.

  4. 4The reason why

    A wrong line still gets its try. The half attempts the landing, the cells that don’t fit glow, and it slides back.

  5. 5Then the beads

    In chapter III, gold beads sit in the cells and each half must hold the same number. Split them unevenly and the heavier half stays down.

  6. 6Hints that wait

    They hold back until you’ve had a real go, then give as little as they can: a matching pair of cells, then the ends of the line, then the line.

Questions

Is Dela free?
Dela is free to download, and chapters I and II are free to play. One purchase unlocks the rest: the later chapters and the original Dela.
I bought Dela before. Do I have to pay again?
No. If you bought Dela before, everything is already yours on the same Apple Account. If something ever shows as locked, open it and tap “Restore purchase”.
What happened to the original game?
It lives on inside Dela as “The original Dela”: all 137 boards, cut in lanes, fewest cuts wins. It is part of the unlock.
When do the next chapters arrive?
Chapter III, Seeds, is part of the unlock at launch. Chapters IV to VII (Apart, Two of everything, Thirds and The exception) arrive in updates in December.
Is there a subscription, ads or tracking?
No. No subscription, no ads, no account. The app collects no data.
Which devices does Dela run on?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 17 or later. More answers on the support page.
