What is Dela

Drag your finger across an abstract shape to cut it into two perfectly balanced halves. Use as few straight lines as possible.

34 hand-crafted curriculum levels, a daily procgen puzzle, and an unlockable endless mode. iPhone and iPad. iOS 17 and later.

No ads. No in-app purchases. No tracking. No accounts. No leaderboards. No push notifications. By design.
