Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026.

Dela is built and operated by Erik Nielsen (“we”).

What we collect

Nothing. Dela does not have user accounts, has no server of its own, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What is stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only, in iOS’s standard UserDefaults storage:

  • Your progress through the chapters and the original Dela.
  • In the original Dela, your best scores and your saved cuts on the board you’re playing.
  • Whether Dela is unlocked on this device.
  • Your in-app preferences.

None of this data leaves your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details or who you are. To decide whether Dela should be unlocked, the app asks Apple’s StoreKit whether your Apple Account owns the unlock or bought Dela before. The answer stays on your device.

Permissions

Dela does not request any system permissions. It does not access your photos, contacts, location, microphone or camera.

While you play, Dela reads how the device is tilted so the light moves across the tiles. This happens on the device only, in the moment, and is never recorded.

Children

Dela does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@sorkila.com.

About this website

This website uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count aggregate visits. Umami does not set cookies, does not identify you, and does not track you across other websites. It respects Do Not Track. The app itself uses no analytics.
