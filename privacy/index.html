Privacy Policy

Effective 1 June 2026.

dela is built and operated by Erik Nielsen (“we”).

What we collect

Nothing. dela does not have user accounts, does not connect to a server, and does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

What is stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only, in iOS’s standard UserDefaults storage:

  • Your level progress and best scores.
  • Your saved cuts on the level you’re currently playing.
  • Your in-app preferences.

None of this data leaves your device. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Permissions

dela does not request any system permissions. It does not access your photos, contacts, location, microphone, camera, or network.

Children

dela does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, email hello@sorkila.com.

About this website

This website uses Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count aggregate visits. Umami does not set cookies, does not identify you, and does not track you across other websites. It respects Do Not Track. The app itself uses no analytics.
